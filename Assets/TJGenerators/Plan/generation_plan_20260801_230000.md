## 7. Risks & Fallbacks

| Risk | Likelihood | Fallback |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Domain reload mid-generation | Medium | C# host re-fires `<bg_task_done>` after recovery; one-time `query_sprite_status` if missing after timeout |
